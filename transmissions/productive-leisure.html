CLASSIFIED TRANSMISSION 002 / RELEASED

The Crime of Productive Leisure

A report on what happens when even a walk needs a target.

FILE 08-A / SECTION 08 · ORION SAINT

The Department has received credible reports that citizens are optimising their rest.

You have a hobby, and the hobby has a spreadsheet. You go for a walk, and the walk has a target. You read a book faster because somewhere you absorbed the idea that books are objects to clear rather than countries in which to become temporarily lost.

Even sleep has become a performance review delivered each morning by a watch that did not sleep at all.
